Foundation

Core Dhikr Formulas

These are the primary words of remembrance — the building blocks of all dhikr. Each one has specific virtues narrated from the Prophet ﷺ.

Tahlil — Declaration of Oneness
Best Dhikr100x Daily
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ
La ilaha illallah
"There is no god but Allah." — The greatest statement a human being can utter. The entire message of every Prophet distilled into one phrase.
When
Anytime. The Prophet ﷺ said it is "the best dhikr" (Tirmidhi 3383). Say it throughout the day, especially in the morning, evening, and after salah. Most emphasised during the last third of the night.
How Often
100x daily specifically narrated. Also: 10x after every salah (Nasa'i). As a general dhikr: unlimited. Some scholars recommend 1,000x or more for seekers on the path.
Benefits
100x/day = reward of freeing 10 slaves, 100 good deeds, 100 sins erased, protection from Shaytan until evening, and no one comes with anything better except one who did more (Bukhari 6403). Best thing said by all Prophets on the Day of Arafah (Tirmidhi 3585).
Source
Bukhari 6403, Muslim 2693, Tirmidhi 3383, 3585
Extended Tahlil — With Praise
100x Daily
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ وَحْدَهُ لَا شَرِيكَ لَهُ، لَهُ الْمُلْكُ وَلَهُ الْحَمْدُ وَهُوَ عَلَىٰ كُلِّ شَيْءٍ قَدِيرٌ
La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahu al-mulku wa lahu al-hamdu wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir
"There is no god but Allah alone, with no partner. His is the sovereignty and His is the praise, and He has power over all things."
When
Morning (after Fajr) and evening. After every salah (1x or 10x). Can be said anytime as general dhikr.
How Often
100x daily for the full reward. 10x = reward of freeing 4 slaves from Isma'il's descendants (Muslim 2693). Also narrated 10x in the morning.
Benefits
100x = equivalent of freeing 10 slaves, 100 hasanat, 100 sins wiped, fortress against Shaytan all day (Bukhari 6403, Muslim 2691). 10x morning = protection and reward of freeing 4 slaves (Ahmad).
Source
Bukhari 6403, Muslim 2691, 2693, Ahmad 8704
Tasbih — Glorification
Beloved to Allah
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ
Subhanallah
"Glory be to Allah" / "Allah is free from all imperfection." — Declaring Allah's transcendence above any deficiency or likeness to creation.
When
After every salah (33x), morning and evening, and anytime throughout the day. Especially when witnessing something awe-inspiring in creation.
How Often
33x after every salah (as part of the tasbih-tahmid-takbir set). Also: "Two words light on the tongue, heavy on the scales" when combined with wa bihamdihi (see below).
Benefits
Each tasbih is a sadaqah (charity) (Muslim 1007). Fills the scales on Judgment Day (Muslim 223). The Prophet ﷺ said: "Subhanallah and Alhamdulillah fill what is between the heavens and earth" (Muslim 223).
Source
Muslim 223, Muslim 1007, Bukhari 843
Tasbih with Praise
100x DailySins Forgiven
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ
Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi
"Glory be to Allah and praise be to Him."
When
Morning and evening especially. Can be said anytime. The Prophet ﷺ specifically loved this dhikr.
How Often
100x daily. The Prophet ﷺ said specifically 100 times a day. Also recommended in the morning and evening.
Benefits
100x/day = all sins forgiven, even if they were like the foam of the sea (Bukhari 6405, Muslim 2691). A palm tree is planted for you in Jannah for each one (Tirmidhi 3464). Whoever says it in the morning and evening 100x, no one comes on Judgment Day with anything better except one who said it more (Muslim 2692).
Source
Bukhari 6405, Muslim 2691, 2692, Tirmidhi 3464
The Two Words — Light yet Heavy
Light on Tongue, Heavy on Scales
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ، سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ
Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi, Subhanallahil-Adhim
"Glory and praise be to Allah. Glory be to Allah, the Magnificent."
When
Anytime. The Prophet ﷺ singled these out as the best words you could say.
How Often
As much as possible. No set limit narrated — the virtue is in their weight, not a specific count.
Benefits
"Two words that are light on the tongue, heavy on the scales, and beloved to al-Rahman" (Bukhari 7563, Muslim 2694). This is the last hadith in Sahih al-Bukhari — Imam Bukhari chose to end his collection with it.
Source
Bukhari 7563, Muslim 2694
Tahmid — Praise
Fills the Scales
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ
Alhamdulillah
"All praise belongs to Allah." — Acknowledging that every blessing, every moment, every breath is from Allah alone.
When
After every salah (33x), after eating, after drinking, after sneezing, upon waking, when receiving good news, when putting on new clothes — and in all circumstances, including hardship.
How Often
33x after every salah. After eating: once. After sneezing: once. As general dhikr: unlimited.
Benefits
"Alhamdulillah fills the scales" (Muslim 223). After eating: "Whoever eats food and says Alhamdulillah... his past sins are forgiven" (Tirmidhi 3458). After sneezing, the response is "Yarhamukallah" — it connects people through mercy.
Source
Muslim 223, Tirmidhi 3458, Bukhari 6224
Takbir — Magnification
34x After Salah
اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Allahu Akbar
"Allah is Greater" — Greater than anything you can conceive, greater than any worry, any desire, any creation. Said at every transition in salah because you are turning away from everything else toward Allah.
When
After every salah (34x, completing the 100 with tasbih and tahmid). During salah at every transition. During the days of Dhul Hijjah and Eid. When climbing a hill during travel.
How Often
34x after every salah (Bukhari 843). During Dhul Hijjah: as much as possible. During travel on high ground.
Benefits
Part of the 33-33-34 formula after salah which equals 99, then complete with La ilaha illallah... = 100. The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever says these after every salah will have all sins forgiven even if they were like the foam of the sea" (Muslim 597).
Source
Muslim 597, Bukhari 843
Hawqalah — Declaration of Dependence
Treasure of Jannah
لَا حَوْلَ وَلَا قُوَّةَ إِلَّا بِاللَّهِ
La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah
"There is no power and no strength except with Allah." — The ultimate declaration of human dependence on Allah. Acknowledging that you cannot even move, think, or breathe without His permission and aid.
When
After every adhan (the mu'adhdhin says "hayya 'ala al-salah" → you respond with this). Also anytime you feel overwhelmed, powerless, or facing a difficult situation. General dhikr anytime.
How Often
After every adhan. The Prophet ﷺ told Abu Musa al-Ash'ari to say it abundantly (Bukhari 4205). No specific count — say it as much as possible.
Benefits
"A treasure from the treasures of Paradise" (Bukhari 4205, Muslim 2704). Cure for 99 diseases, the least of which is worry (Ibn Abi al-Dunya). It is the essence of tawakkul in verbal form.
Source
Bukhari 4205, Muslim 2704
Istighfar — Seeking Forgiveness
100x DailyOpens Provision
أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ
Astaghfirullah
"I seek forgiveness from Allah." — Not just for sins you know about, but for shortcomings you don't even recognise. The Prophet ﷺ sought forgiveness 70-100 times every single day — and he was sinless.
When
After every salah (3x, Sunnah to say immediately after salam). Throughout the day — especially after sinning, before sleeping, and in the last third of the night. Also at gatherings as a closing (kaffarah al-majlis).
How Often
3x after every salah. The Prophet ﷺ said: "I seek forgiveness from Allah more than 70 times a day" (Bukhari 6307). Minimum: 100x daily recommended by scholars.
Benefits
"Whoever makes istighfar a constant practice, Allah will make for him a way out of every difficulty, relief from every anxiety, and provision from where he does not expect" (Abu Dawud 1518). Nuh told his people: "Seek forgiveness... He will send rain, give you wealth and children, and grant you gardens and rivers" (71:10-12).
Source
Bukhari 6307, Abu Dawud 1518, Muslim 591, Qur'an 71:10-12
Sayyid al-Istighfar — The Master of Forgiveness
Best IstighfarGuaranteed Jannah
اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ رَبِّي لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ، خَلَقْتَنِي وَأَنَا عَبْدُكَ، وَأَنَا عَلَى عَهْدِكَ وَوَعْدِكَ مَا اسْتَطَعْتُ، أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا صَنَعْتُ، أَبُوءُ لَكَ بِنِعْمَتِكَ عَلَيَّ وَأَبُوءُ بِذَنْبِي، فَاغْفِرْ لِي فَإِنَّهُ لَا يَغْفِرُ الذُّنُوبَ إِلَّا أَنْتَ
Allahumma anta Rabbi la ilaha illa anta, khalaqtani wa ana 'abduka, wa ana 'ala 'ahdika wa wa'dika mastata'tu, a'udhu bika min sharri ma sana'tu, abu'u laka bini'matika 'alayya wa abu'u bidhanbi, faghfir li fa innahu la yaghfiru al-dhunuba illa anta
"O Allah, You are my Lord. There is no god but You. You created me and I am Your servant. I uphold Your covenant and promise as best I can. I seek refuge in You from the evil of what I have done. I acknowledge Your blessings upon me, and I acknowledge my sins. So forgive me, for none forgives sins except You."
When
Morning (after Fajr) and evening (after Asr/Maghrib). The Prophet ﷺ specifically linked its virtue to these two times.
How Often
1x morning and 1x evening. Quality over quantity — say it with understanding and conviction, not rushing.
Benefits
"Whoever says it during the day with conviction and dies that day before evening, he is from the people of Jannah. Whoever says it at night with conviction and dies before morning, he is from the people of Jannah" (Bukhari 6306). This is one of the strongest guarantees in any hadith.
Source
Bukhari 6306
Salawat — Blessings upon the Prophet ﷺ
MandatoryFriday: Abundant
اللَّهُمَّ صَلِّ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا صَلَّيْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ. اللَّهُمَّ بَارِكْ عَلَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَعَلَى آلِ مُحَمَّدٍ كَمَا بَارَكْتَ عَلَى إِبْرَاهِيمَ وَعَلَى آلِ إِبْرَاهِيمَ إِنَّكَ حَمِيدٌ مَجِيدٌ
Allahumma salli 'ala Muhammad wa 'ala ali Muhammad, kama sallayta 'ala Ibrahim wa 'ala ali Ibrahim, innaka Hamidun Majid. Allahumma barik 'ala Muhammad wa 'ala ali Muhammad, kama barakta 'ala Ibrahim wa 'ala ali Ibrahim, innaka Hamidun Majid.
The Ibrahimi Salawat — the full form taught by the Prophet ﷺ himself when the Companions asked how to send blessings upon him.
When
In tashahud (obligatory). After adhan. Abundantly on Friday — the best day of the week. When his name is mentioned. When making du'a (before and after). When entering and leaving the mosque.
How Often
10x morning and 10x evening (minimum recommended). On Friday: as much as possible. Short form: Sallallahu 'alayhi wa sallam. Scholars recommend 100x daily or more.
Benefits
1x salawat = Allah sends 10 blessings upon you, erases 10 sins, raises you 10 degrees (Muslim 384). Closest people to the Prophet on Judgment Day are those who sent the most salawat (Tirmidhi 484). Du'a is suspended between heaven and earth until you send salawat (Tirmidhi 486). "The miser is the one who hears my name and does not send salawat" (Tirmidhi 3546).
Source
Muslim 384, Tirmidhi 484, 486, 3546, Abu Dawud 1047
Basmalah — In the Name of Allah
Before Every Action
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ
Bismillah
"In the name of Allah." — Invoking Allah's name before every action, consecrating it and seeking His blessing and protection in it.
When
Before eating, drinking, entering the house, before wudu, before intimacy, when starting any task, when boarding a vehicle, when beginning a letter or document. Essentially: before everything.
How Often
Every single time you begin an action. It is Sunnah before eating (and some scholars say obligatory before slaughtering). Full form before eating: Bismillah. Full form at start of Qur'anic surahs: Bismillahi al-Rahmani al-Rahim.
Benefits
Prevents Shaytan from sharing your food (Muslim 2017). Protects the home when said upon entering (Abu Dawud 3765). Shaytan says to his companions: "You have no lodging for the night and no dinner" when Bismillah is said (Muslim 2018). General protection and barakah in every action.
Source
Muslim 2017, 2018, Abu Dawud 3765
Hasbunallah — Allah is Sufficient
In Times of Trial
حَسْبُنَا اللَّهُ وَنِعْمَ الْوَكِيلُ
Hasbunallahu wa ni'mal wakil
"Allah is sufficient for us, and He is the best Disposer of affairs."
When
When facing fear, difficulty, opposition, or overwhelming circumstances. Said by Ibrahim when thrown into the fire. Said by the Companions at the Battle of Uhud when told "the people have gathered against you" (3:173). Morning and evening adhkar (7x).
How Often
7x morning and 7x evening (narrated from the Prophet ﷺ, Abu Dawud). Also in any moment of distress or fear — no limit.
Benefits
"Allah will suffice you" — a direct promise when said with conviction. Ibrahim said it and the fire became cool and safe (21:69). The Companions said it at Uhud and "they returned with grace and bounty from Allah" (3:174). 7x morning/evening: "Allah will suffice him in all that concerns him" (Abu Dawud).
Source
Qur'an 3:173-174, Bukhari 4563, Abu Dawud (morning/evening narration)
The Eternal Goodness — al-Baqiyat al-Salihat
Best Speech After Qur'an
سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ، وَالْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ، وَلَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا اللَّهُ، وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ
Subhanallah, wal-hamdulillah, wa la ilaha illallah, wallahu akbar
"Glory be to Allah, praise be to Allah, there is no god but Allah, and Allah is the Greatest." — The four combined are called al-Baqiyat al-Salihat (the Enduring Good Deeds, referenced in Qur'an 18:46).
When
Anytime — these are the best words after the Qur'an. Say them together as a set. Morning and evening. Before sleep. As a continuous dhikr throughout the day.
How Often
No specific limit for the combined set. The Prophet ﷺ said they are more beloved to him than everything the sun rises upon (Muslim 2695). Say them as much as possible.
Benefits
"The most beloved speech to Allah is four: Subhanallah, Alhamdulillah, La ilaha illallah, Allahu Akbar. It does not matter which you begin with" (Muslim 2137). "More beloved to me than everything the sun rises upon" (Muslim 2695). They are the al-Baqiyat al-Salihat that remain when all worldly things perish (18:46).
Source
Muslim 2137, 2695, Qur'an 18:46
Divine Names

Names of Allah in Dhikr

Some of Allah's names have specific dhikr formulas and virtues attached to them in the Sunnah.

Ya Hayyu Ya Qayyum — The Living, The Sustainer
Ism al-A'dham
يَا حَيُّ يَا قَيُّومُ بِرَحْمَتِكَ أَسْتَغِيثُ
Ya Hayyu Ya Qayyum, bi rahmatika astaghith
"O Ever-Living, O Sustainer, by Your mercy I seek help."
When
In times of distress. Before du'a. The Prophet ﷺ would say this when something distressed him. Some scholars say al-Hayy al-Qayyum is part of Allah's Greatest Name (Ism al-A'dham).
Benefits
The Prophet ﷺ frequently used this in du'a. Contains what some scholars identify as al-Ism al-A'dham — the Greatest Name of Allah by which du'a is always answered (Tirmidhi 3524). Anas said: "When any matter grieved the Prophet, he would say: Ya Hayyu Ya Qayyum..." (Tirmidhi 3524).
Source
Tirmidhi 3524, Hakim 1/509
Ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram
Persist in This
يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ
Ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram
"O Possessor of Majesty and Honour."
When
In du'a. The Prophet ﷺ specifically said: "Persist in [saying]: Ya Dhal-Jalali wal-Ikram" (Tirmidhi 3524, Nasa'i). Also the last verse of Surah al-Rahman (55:78).
Benefits
The Prophet ﷺ commanded persistence in this particular invocation. It combines both aspects of Allah — His overwhelming Majesty (Jalal) and His generous Honour (Ikram). Reciting it connects you to both fear and hope simultaneously.
Source
Tirmidhi 3524, Nasa'i in al-Sunan al-Kubra

Evening

Evening Adhkar (Adhkar al-Masa')

Said after Asr prayer (or after Maghrib according to some scholars) until sleep. The same formulas as the morning adhkar with "amsayna" replacing "asbahna." A few have specific evening additions.

💡

Note: Almost all morning adhkar are repeated in the evening with the wording adjusted (e.g., "we have entered the evening" instead of "we have entered the morning"). The table above applies to both. Below are the evening-specific additions.

Evening-Specific: Last Two Verses of Al-Baqarah
Night Protection
آمَنَ الرَّسُولُ بِمَا أُنزِلَ إِلَيْهِ مِن رَّبِّهِ وَالْمُؤْمِنُونَ...
Surah Al-Baqarah 2:285-286
The final two verses of Surah al-Baqarah — a comprehensive declaration of faith, obedience, and du'a for ease.
When
At night before sleeping. The Prophet ﷺ said these two verses are sufficient for whoever recites them at night.
Benefits
"Whoever recites the last two verses of Surah al-Baqarah at night, they will be sufficient for him" (Bukhari 5009) — sufficient as protection from all evil, sufficient in reward, sufficient in every need. They were given to the Prophet on the Night Journey (Isra'), not through Jibril in the usual way.
Source
Bukhari 5009, Muslim 807
Evening-Specific: Surah Al-Mulk
Shield from Grave
تَبَارَكَ الَّذِي بِيَدِهِ الْمُلْكُ...
Surah Al-Mulk (67) — 30 verses
The Surah of Sovereignty — recite the entire surah before sleeping.
When
Every night before sleeping.
Benefits
"A surah of 30 verses interceded for a man until he was forgiven" (Tirmidhi 2891, Abu Dawud 1400). It protects from the punishment of the grave. The Prophet ﷺ would not sleep until he recited it (Tirmidhi 2892).
Source
Tirmidhi 2891, 2892, Abu Dawud 1400
After Salah

Adhkar After Every Salah

The moments after salah are among the most powerful times for dhikr. The Prophet ﷺ had a specific routine he followed after every obligatory prayer.

OrderDhikrCountNote
1 أَسْتَغْفِرُ اللَّهَ Astaghfirullah 3x Immediately after salam — seeking forgiveness for any shortcomings in the prayer itself
2 اللَّهُمَّ أَنْتَ السَّلَامُ وَمِنْكَ السَّلَامُ تَبَارَكْتَ يَا ذَا الْجَلَالِ وَالْإِكْرَامِ "O Allah, You are Peace, from You is peace. Blessed are You, O Possessor of Majesty and Honour." 1x Muslim 591. Said immediately after salam before anything else.
3 Ayat al-Kursi (2:255) 1x "Nothing prevents him from entering Jannah except death" (Nasa'i in al-Kubra)
4 سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ Subhanallah 33x The famous 33-33-34 set. Equals 99. Then complete 100 with the extended tahlil. "Sins forgiven even if like foam of the sea" (Muslim 597). Can also be done 10-10-10 (Muslim 597) or 33-33-33 then 1x tahlil = 100.
5 الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ Alhamdulillah 33x
6 اللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ Allahu Akbar 34x
7 Extended Tahlil: La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah... 1x Completes the 100. The seal of the post-salah dhikr.
8 Surah Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, Al-Nas 1x each Recommended after every salah. After Maghrib and Fajr: 3x each (Tirmidhi 3575).
The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever glorifies Allah (subhanallah) 33 times after every salah, praises Allah (alhamdulillah) 33 times, and magnifies Allah (Allahu akbar) 34 times — that is 100 — then says 'La ilaha illallahu wahdahu la sharika lah, lahu al-mulku wa lahu al-hamdu wa huwa 'ala kulli shay'in qadir' — his sins will be forgiven even if they were like the foam of the sea."
Sahih Muslim 597
Sleep

Before Sleep

The Prophet ﷺ had a detailed routine before sleeping — dhikr, du'a, and specific surahs that protect during the vulnerable hours of sleep.

PracticeDetailsBenefit
Ayat al-Kursi Recite once "A protector from Allah will be assigned to you and no devil will come near you until morning" (Bukhari 2311 — the hadith of Abu Hurayrah and the jinn)
Al-Ikhlas, Al-Falaq, Al-Nas Recite each, blow into cupped hands, wipe over body — 3x The Prophet ﷺ did this every night (Bukhari 5017). Complete protection from evil.
Last 2 verses of Al-Baqarah Recite 2:285-286 "Sufficient for him" — in protection and reward (Bukhari 5009)
Surah Al-Mulk (67) Recite full surah Intercedes until forgiven. Protects from grave punishment (Tirmidhi 2891).
Surah Al-Sajdah (32) + Al-Mulk Both recited together "The Prophet ﷺ would not sleep until he recited Alif-Lam-Mim Tanzil (32) and Tabarak (67)" (Tirmidhi 2892)
Surah Al-Kafirun (109) Recite once "Recite it before sleeping — it is a disavowal of shirk" (Tirmidhi 3403, Abu Dawud 5055)
بِاسْمِكَ اللَّهُمَّ أَمُوتُ وَأَحْيَا
"In Your name, O Allah, I die and I live"		 1x when lying down Acknowledging that sleep is a minor death and waking is a resurrection. Puts your soul in Allah's care (Bukhari 6324).
Tasbih (33), Tahmid (33), Takbir (34) Total = 100 The "Fatimah dhikr" — the Prophet ﷺ taught Fatimah this instead of a servant, saying "This is better for you" (Bukhari 3705). Gives strength for the next day.
Wudu before sleep Make wudu as for prayer "When you go to bed, make wudu as for prayer, then lie on your right side" (Bukhari 247). An angel makes du'a for you all night.
Dust off bed 3x With the edge of your garment "For he does not know what came onto it after him" (Bukhari 6320). Sunnah practice to clean the sleeping area.
Waking

Upon Waking

The very first words and actions after waking set the tone for the entire day.

First Words Upon Waking
Sunnah
الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ الَّذِي أَحْيَانَا بَعْدَمَا أَمَاتَنَا وَإِلَيْهِ النُّشُورُ
Alhamdulillahil-ladhi ahyana ba'dama amatana wa ilayhin-nushur
"All praise is for Allah who gave us life after having taken it from us, and to Him is the resurrection."
When
The very first thing you say when you open your eyes. Before getting out of bed. This was the Prophet's practice (Bukhari 6324).
Benefits
Begins your day with gratitude and awareness that sleep was a minor death. Sets the spiritual tone — your first conscious thought is of Allah. Fulfils the Sunnah and earns reward from the start of the day.
Source
Bukhari 6324
💡

After this, proceed with wudu and then the morning adhkar after Fajr prayer. The Sunnah also includes using the miswak upon waking (Bukhari 889).

Protection

Distress, Anxiety & Protection

The Prophet ﷺ taught specific adhkar for moments of worry, fear, sadness, and difficulty. These are spiritual medicine.

Du'a for Anxiety and Sorrow
Distress Relief
اللَّهُمَّ إِنِّي أَعُوذُ بِكَ مِنَ الْهَمِّ وَالْحَزَنِ، وَالْعَجْزِ وَالْكَسَلِ، وَالْبُخْلِ وَالْجُبْنِ، وَضَلَعِ الدَّيْنِ وَغَلَبَةِ الرِّجَالِ
Allahumma inni a'udhu bika min al-hammi wal-hazan, wal-'ajzi wal-kasal, wal-bukhli wal-jubn, wa dala'i al-dayni wa ghalabat al-rijal
"O Allah, I seek refuge in You from worry and grief, from inability and laziness, from miserliness and cowardice, from the burden of debt and the overpowering of men."
When
Whenever feeling anxious, worried, overwhelmed, or depressed. Anas said the Prophet ﷺ used to say this frequently (Bukhari 2893).
Benefits
Covers 8 afflictions in 4 pairs — past grief & future worry, inability & laziness, miserliness & cowardice, debt & domination. A comprehensive shield against every form of psychological distress.
Source
Bukhari 2893, 6369
Du'a of Yunus (in the Whale)
Never Fails
لَا إِلَٰهَ إِلَّا أَنتَ سُبْحَانَكَ إِنِّي كُنتُ مِنَ الظَّالِمِينَ
La ilaha illa anta subhanaka inni kuntu min al-dhalimin
"There is no god but You. Glory be to You. Indeed, I have been among the wrongdoers." — The du'a of Prophet Yunus from the belly of the whale.
When
Any moment of distress, difficulty, or when feeling trapped in a situation with no way out. The beauty is it combines tawhid, tasbih, and confession of shortcoming — all the elements of a perfect du'a.
Benefits
"No Muslim calls upon Allah with this du'a regarding any matter except that Allah answers him" (Tirmidhi 3505). The Prophet ﷺ gave a universal guarantee — it works for any matter, not just extreme distress.
Source
Qur'an 21:87, Tirmidhi 3505
When Struck by Calamity
Replaced with Better
إِنَّا لِلَّهِ وَإِنَّا إِلَيْهِ رَاجِعُونَ. اللَّهُمَّ أْجُرْنِي فِي مُصِيبَتِي وَأَخْلِفْ لِي خَيْرًا مِنْهَا
Inna lillahi wa inna ilayhi raji'un. Allahumma ajurni fi musibati wa akhlif li khayran minha
"To Allah we belong and to Him we return. O Allah, reward me in my calamity and replace it with something better."
When
At the moment of any loss, hardship, or calamity — death, financial loss, illness, failure, heartbreak. Say it immediately when the news hits.
Benefits
Umm Salamah narrated this — when Abu Salamah died, she said it, wondering "who could be better than Abu Salamah?" Allah replaced him with the Prophet Muhammad ﷺ himself as her husband. "Whoever says this, Allah will reward him in his affliction and replace it with something better" (Muslim 918).
Source
Muslim 918
Protection from Evil Eye
Daily Shield
أَعُوذُ بِكَلِمَاتِ اللَّهِ التَّامَّاتِ مِنْ شَرِّ مَا خَلَقَ
A'udhu bikalimatillahi al-tammati min sharri ma khalaq
"I seek refuge in the perfect words of Allah from the evil of what He has created."
When
Evening. When staying somewhere (travel, hotel, new place). The Prophet ﷺ said: "Whoever stops at a place and says this, nothing will harm him until he leaves that place" (Muslim 2708).
How Often
3x evening. Also when entering any new place or feeling unsafe.
Benefits
Complete protection from evil — including jinn, evil eye, scorpion/snake, and all creation. The Prophet ﷺ used to say this over Hasan and Husayn for protection (Bukhari 3371).
Source
Muslim 2708, Bukhari 3371
Practice

Building Your Wird (Daily Litany)

A wird is your fixed daily spiritual practice — the non-negotiable minimum you do every single day. The scholars say: "The wird is not what you do when you have time. It is what you do before everything else."

💡

The Prophet's Advice: "The most beloved deeds to Allah are the most consistent, even if small" (Bukhari 6464). Choose a wird you can sustain forever — even on your worst day. Then never leave it. Better to do a small amount consistently for 40 years than to do a large amount for 2 weeks and stop.

LevelDaily WirdTime Needed
Beginner After each salah: 33 Subhanallah + 33 Alhamdulillah + 34 Allahu Akbar + 1x Extended Tahlil. Morning: Ayat al-Kursi + 3x Ikhlas/Falaq/Nas. 100x Astaghfirullah anytime. 100x Salawat anytime. ~15-20 min total
Intermediate All of the above + Full morning adhkar + Full evening adhkar + 100x La ilaha illallah + 100x Subhanallahi wa bihamdihi + Before-sleep adhkar (Mulk + last 2 of Baqarah + Ikhlas/Falaq/Nas) ~30-40 min total
Advanced All of the above + Tahajjud (even 2 rak'at) + 1 juz' Qur'an daily + 1,000x La ilaha illallah + 1,000x Salawat + Muhasabah journal before sleep + Muraqabah (5-10 min contemplative presence) ~1.5-2 hours total
لَا تَتْرُكِ الذِّكْرَ لِعَدَمِ حُضُورِكَ مَعَ اللَّهِ فِيهِ، لِأَنَّ غَفْلَتَكَ عَنْ وُجُودِ ذِكْرِهِ أَشَدُّ مِنْ غَفْلَتِكَ فِي وُجُودِ ذِكْرِهِ
"Do not abandon dhikr because you do not feel present with Allah in it, for your heedlessness without dhikr is worse than your heedlessness during dhikr. — Ibn 'Ata'illah al-Iskandari, al-Hikam (#67)
